# User Guide: HH - Banner Component

The HH - Banner is a customizable web component used to create visually appealing banners on your website. It displays a background element such as a video, an image, or a flat color, along with a main heading. Use it to highlight key messages or visual content and make your web pages more engaging.

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Editing

1. **Media Type (Required):**  
   Choose the type of background from the dropdown menu:  
   - **Video**: Displays a video in the background.  
   - **Image**: Displays an image in the background.  
   - **None**: Uses a flat color background.  

   👉 *Tip: Select the option that best matches your design needs.*

2. **Heading (Required):**  
   Enter the main text for the banner. This is the large heading users will see over the background. Keep it short and impactful.  
   👉 *Tip: Aim for 3–5 words that grab attention.*

3. **Conditional Fields:**  
   - **If Video is selected**: Provide a link to the internal video asset in the *Video Source* field.  
     👉 *Tip: Ensure the video is high quality and aligns with your content theme.*  
   - **If Image is selected**: Choose an image using the *Image* field.  
     👉 *Tip: Use a high-resolution image that works well with the heading text's color and placement.*

## Best Practices

- Always fill in the required fields (**Media Type** and **Heading**) for the banner to display properly.  
- For videos and images, check the file quality to prevent blurry visuals.  
- Keep your heading concise and choose contrasting colors to ensure text is easy to read over the background.  

That's it! With just a few clicks, you'll have a banner that enhances your content and catches the viewer's attention.
