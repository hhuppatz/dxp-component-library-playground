# User Guide: HH - Accordion Component

The **HH - Accordion** component is a sleek, interactive feature that displays headings with hidden content beneath them. Users can click on a heading to reveal its related content, making it perfect for FAQs, step-by-step guides, or organizing detailed information in a space-saving way. On the page, it looks like a series of headings stacked vertically, with each click expanding to show the associated content. This helps make your content more engaging and user-friendly.

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Editing the HH - Accordion

### 1. **Title (Optional)**
   - This is the heading displayed above the entire accordion.
   - **What to do:** Enter a short, descriptive title. For example, "Frequently Asked Questions" or "How It Works."
   - Tip: Keep it concise but informative to give users a clear idea of what the accordion is about.

### 2. **Accordion Items (Required)**
   - Each accordion item consists of a **Heading** and its corresponding **Content**.
   - You can add between **1 and 20 items**.

   #### For each accordion item:
   - **Heading (Required):** Type the title for the accordion section.  
     Example: "What is included in this package?"
   - **Content (Required):** Enter the text that should be displayed when the heading is clicked.  
     Example: "This package includes templates, user guides, and free updates for one year."  
     Tip: Use clear and concise language for easy readability.
   
   - To add more items, click the “Add Item” button and repeat this process for each section. To remove an item, simply delete it.

## Tips for Success
- Use consistent phrasing for headings to maintain a professional look.
- Ensure all headings and content are clear and help answer user questions or provide value.
- Avoid adding too much content in one section to keep it easy to scan for users. If needed, break it into multiple accordion items.
- Test your accordion after editing to ensure it expands and collapses as expected and displays helpful, accurate content.

With these steps, you'll have an engaging accordion that organizes your content beautifully for your audience!
