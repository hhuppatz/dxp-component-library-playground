# User Guide: HH - Block Quote

The **HH - Block Quote** component showcases a styled quote on a webpage, making it perfect for highlighting impactful statements or inspirational words. This component includes the quote’s text, the author's name, and an optional title to introduce the quote. It helps add personality and context to your content.

## How to Edit the HH - Block Quote Component

Follow these simple steps to customize this component:

1. **Title** (Optional): Add a short title above the quote to give it context. For example, "Customer Testimonial" or "Words of Wisdom." If you don't need a title, you can leave this blank — the default title will not display unless edited.

2. **Quote** (Required): Enter the text of the quote. This field is mandatory, as it's the main focus of the component. Aim for concise, meaningful quotes that resonate with your audience. For example: "The only limit to our realization of tomorrow is our doubts of today."

3. **Author** (Optional): Add the name of the person who said the quote. If the quote comes from an anonymous source, you can leave this blank or use a placeholder like "Unknown." 

4. **Favourite Coffee** (Optional): If you'd like, you can include a whimsical note such as the user’s favorite coffee. This field is purely optional and is not displayed on the webpage unless filled in. Feel free to leave this blank unless it's relevant to your use case.

### Tips for Optimal Results

- **Choose impactful quotes**: Short, thought-provoking or relatable quotes work best. Avoid very long quotes, as they may be harder to read.
- **Be consistent**: If you're using multiple block quotes across the website, keep formatting and authorship consistent for visual harmony and trustworthiness.
- **Preview your work**: Always preview your changes to ensure the visual appearance matches your expectations.

With these steps and tips, your quotes can become a standout feature on your site that leaves a lasting impression!
