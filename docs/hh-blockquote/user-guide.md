# User Guide: HH - Block Quote Component

The **HH - Block Quote** component displays a quote on your webpage, paired with the author's name. It may also include an optional title above the quote. This is perfect for highlighting impactful sayings, testimonials, or notable excerpts to engage your audience visually and emotionally.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Editing

### 1. **Title** *(Optional)*
Enter a title for the section above your quote. If left blank, the default "Section title" will display. Keep your title short and relevant for clarity. Example: *"Thoughts of Great Minds."*

### 2. **Quote** *(Required)*
This field contains the text of the quote. Write or paste the quote you want to highlight, keeping it impactful and concise. **This field must be filled for the component to display properly**. Example: *"The only limit to our realization of tomorrow is our doubts of today."*

### 3. **Author** *(Optional)*
Add the name of the person who wrote or said the quote. If left blank, the default "Author" will display. Adding an author lends credibility and context to your quote. Example: *"Franklin D. Roosevelt."*

### 4. **Favourite Coffee** *(Optional and unrelated)*
This field allows you to specify the author's favourite coffee, but it is not displayed on the page. You can leave this blank unless needed for internal purposes.

---

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Keep Titles Focused**: Titles are optional but can help set the context. Ensure they align with the theme of your quote.
- **Curate the Quote Thoughtfully**: Choose quotes that resonate with your audience, are easy to understand, and fit the purpose of your page.
- **Credibility Matters**: Adding an author's name (if known) makes the quote more impactful.
- **Preview Before Publishing**: Make use of the inline editing feature to tweak your fields directly on the page for a seamless presentation.

---

With this guide, you can effortlessly add beautifully displayed block quotes to your content using the HH - Block Quote component.
