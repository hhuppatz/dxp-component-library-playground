# User Guide: Block Quote Component

The **Block Quote** component is perfect for showcasing a meaningful quote along with its author’s name. It can also include a title above the quote for added context. This visually highlights important or impactful text in a way that stands out on your webpage, making it ideal for testimonials, featured quotes, or inspirational statements.

### How to Fill in the Fields

1. **Title (optional)**  
   - This is the heading that appears above the quote (e.g., "What Our Clients Say").
   - To customize the title, simply type your text in the field.
   - If you prefer not to have a title, you can leave this empty.

2. **Quote (required)**  
   - Enter the actual content of the quote—this is the main focus of the block.  
   - Example: *"The only limit to our realization of tomorrow is our doubts of today."*  
   - *Tip:* Keep the quote short and impactful for better readability.

3. **Author (optional)**  
   - Add the name of the person who said the quote, like "Franklin D. Roosevelt."
   - If the quote doesn’t need attribution, leave this field blank.

4. **Favourite Coffee (optional)**  
   - This quirky optional field lets you add the author's favorite coffee. While it’s included for fun and experimentation, it’s typically unnecessary for a professional block quote. You can leave it empty unless you want to display it on the page.

### Best Practices
- For the most polished look, pair short, powerful quotes with concise author details.
- Stick to a single, impactful idea per quote for better emphasis.
- The **Quote** field must always be completed, while other fields can be left blank if they aren’t relevant.

That’s it! With these simple steps, you can effortlessly elevate your webpage using the Block Quote component.
