# User Guide: Editing a Card Component in Squiz Matrix

The **Card Component** is a versatile block used to showcase information on your webpage in a visually appealing format. It typically includes a heading, supporting text, an optional background image, and a clickable link. You might use cards to highlight articles, services, or key messages. On the page, a card looks like a compact content box that grabs attention and invites users to engage.

## How to Edit the Card Component

Follow these steps to update the card:

### 1. **Content Type** *(optional)*  
   - **What it is:** A label displayed above the card's heading, such as "News" or "Blog Post."  
   - **How to use:** Click into the field and add a short descriptor that summarizes the card's context. Keep it brief for clarity.  

### 2. **Heading** *(required)*  
   - **What it is:** The main title of the card.  
   - **How to use:** Enter a clear and engaging headline that captures the card's purpose. Aim for concise wording (ideally under 10 words).  

### 3. **Supporting Text** *(optional)*  
   - **What it is:** Text below the heading that provides more details or context.  
   - **How to use:** Add a short paragraph or sentence to entice users to take action or learn more. Avoid long blocks of text for better readability.  

### 4. **Link** *(optional)*  
   - **What it is:** A clickable link that redirects users to another page or resource.  
   - **How to use:** Add a URL and ensure your link relates to the content in the card. Use descriptive link text like "Read more" instead of a long URL.  

### 5. **Image** *(optional)*  
   - **What it is:** A background image to visually enhance the card.  
   - **How to use:** Choose a high-quality image that aligns with your content. Keep it simple and ensure it doesn't overshadow the text.

## Best Practices

- **Required Field:** Always fill in the heading field since it’s mandatory. Without it, the card won’t work.  
- **Avoid Clutter:** Stick to a maximum of 2–3 sentences in the Supporting Text for a clean and professional look.  
- **Image Use:** If using a background image, pick one that complements the text and doesn’t distract from readability.  
- **Consistency:** Match the tone and style of all fields with your website’s branding to maintain cohesiveness.

Take advantage of the preview feature in Squiz Matrix to double-check how the card looks before publishing.
