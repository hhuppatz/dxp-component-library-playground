# User Guide for Editing the HH - Card Component

The **HH - Card** is a visual building block used to display content on your webpage. It’s typically used to highlight key information such as articles, services, or announcements in a compact, visually appealing card format. It includes a heading, optional supporting text, a link, and a background image.

Here’s how to edit the **HH - Card** step-by-step:

---

### 1. **Content Type** (Optional)
- This is a brief label displayed above the card's heading.  
- Example: "News", "Event", "Tip".  
- You can leave it blank if you don’t need this label.  

---

### 2. **Heading** (Required)
- The main text of your card—make it clear and engaging.  
- Example: "Join Our Webinar" or "Read Our Latest Blog Post".  
- **Tip:** Keep it concise and to the point, ideally under 8 words.

---

### 3. **Supporting Text** (Optional)  
- Additional details or descriptions displayed below the heading.  
- Example: "Learn new skills in our free online webinar."  
- **Tip:** It’s best to keep this text short and readable.

---

### 4. **Link** (Optional)
- Add a clickable link that takes users to a page with more information.  
- Include the full URL or link to an internal page in your site.  
- **Best Practice:** Make sure the link is relevant to the heading and supporting text.

---

### 5. **Image** (Optional)
- Upload an image for the card background. This makes your card visually appealing.  
- Use high-quality images that match the content of your card.  
- **Best Practice:** Ensure the image is not too busy or text-heavy to maintain readability.

---

### Final Tips:
- Only the **Heading** is required; the other fields can be left blank if they’re not needed.
- Review your card in the page preview to see how it looks once it’s published.
- Pick an image and text that align in tone and message for a better user experience.

By using these steps, you’ll create impactful and user-friendly HH - Cards that enhance your website!
