# User Guide: Editing the Accordion Component in Squiz Matrix

The **Accordion** component is a great way to organize content on your website. It displays a list of headings, and when a visitor clicks on a heading, the corresponding hidden content is revealed. This makes it ideal for FAQs, collapsible sections, or any scenario where you want to display information in a compact, user-friendly way.

## How to Edit the Accordion Component

Follow these steps to customize the Accordion component:

1. **Title (Optional)**  
   Add a title that will appear above the accordion. This could be a heading like "Frequently Asked Questions". If you leave it blank, a default title ("Section title") will be used.

2. **Accordion Items (Required)**  
   - **Add at Least One Item**  
     You must create at least one accordion item, but you can add up to 20. Click the "Add Accordion Item" button to get started.
   - **Heading (Required)**  
     Enter a clear and concise heading for each item, such as "What is your return policy?". This is what users will click to expand the content.
   - **Content (Required)**  
     Enter detailed content that will appear when the accordion is expanded. Keep it clear and to the point. You can use formatting (e.g., bold, bullet points) to make the content more engaging and easy to read.

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Be Concise:** For the headings, use short phrases that immediately explain what the section is about. Avoid long sentences.
- **Organize Logically:** Place the most important or commonly accessed items at the top. Consider grouping similar topics together.
- **Preview Your Changes:** After making edits, preview the page to ensure your accordion works well and that all content fits neatly.
- **Stick to the Limits:** Remember, you can only have between 1 and 20 accordion items—avoid oversaturating the component with too many items.

By following these instructions and tips, you’ll create a functional and user-friendly Accordion component for your website. Happy editing!
