# User Guide for Editing the "Icon Cards" Component

Welcome to the "Icon Cards" component! This guide will help you understand what the component looks like, how to edit it effectively, and how to make the most of its features. Let’s dive in!

---

## What Are "Icon Cards"?  

The "Icon Cards" component displays a collection of visually appealing cards on a web page. Each card can include:  
- An **icon** to make it visually engaging.  
- A **heading** to describe the card’s purpose.  
- **Text content** to provide more information.  
- An optional **link** for users to take action or learn more.  

"Icon Cards" are great for summarizing key services, steps, or features in a concise and aesthetically pleasing way. Here’s an example layout:  

```
SECTION TITLE
[ Icon ]    [ Icon ]    [ Icon ]  
Heading 1   Heading 2   Heading 3  
Text 1      Text 2      Text 3  
Link        Link        Link  
```  
You can customize the number of columns (2, 3, or 4) to suit your design preferences.

---

## How to Edit the "Icon Cards" Component  

Follow these steps to edit each section of the component:

### 1. **Editing the Section Title**
   - Click on the field labeled **Section Title**.
   - Enter the text to display above the cards (e.g., "Our Services" or "Key Features"). This will act as a heading for the entire section.
   - _(Required)_ You must provide a Section Title, or the component will not display properly.

   **Tip:** Keep this short and descriptive—just a few words to summarize the section's purpose.

---

### 2. **Adding or Editing Cards**
You need at least one card in this component to display content. Each card has a few fields for customization:  

#### 2.1 **Icon (Required)**  
   - Choose an icon that represents the card's content.
   - Click the dropdown next to **Icon Class**, and select one of the following options:  
     - **paintBucket**  
     - **pen**  
     - **printer**  
     - **tools**  

   **Tip:** Match the icon to the card’s content to create visual clarity (e.g., use "printer" for printing services).  

#### 2.2 **Heading (Optional)**  
   - Click the field labeled **Heading**, and type the card's headline.  
   - This can be a short phrase or keyword that summarizes the card's topic.   

   **Tip:** Keep headings concise—ideally fewer than 5 words.  

#### 2.3 **Content (Required)**  
   - Add the main text describing this card in the **Content** field.  
   - Keep it brief but informative, typically 1–3 sentences.  

   **Tip:** Use clear and simple language that visitors can understand at a glance.  

#### 2.4 **Link (Optional)**  
   - Enter the URL or select an internal page to link the card to in the **Link** field.  
   - If you don’t need a link, you can leave this field empty.  

   **Tip:** Links are great for leading users to more detailed content or actions—use them when necessary, but don’t clutter the design.

---

### 3. **Adding More Cards**  
   - Click **+ Add another Icon Card** to add a new card.
   - Fill out the fields for the new card by following steps 2.1–2.4 above.

   **Note:** The "Icon Cards" component requires at least **one card**, but you can add as many as you need.

---

### 4. **Configuring the Layout**  
   - Scroll down to the **Icon Cards Configuration** section.  
   - Under **Number of Columns**, select how many columns you’d like to display:
     - **2 Columns**  
     - **3 Columns**  
     - **4 Columns** _(Default: 2 Columns)_

   **Tip:** Choose the number of columns based on your content and page width:  
   - 2 Columns: Minimal and spacious layout.  
   - 3 Columns: Balanced for most screens.  
   - 4 Columns: Best for compact designs with lots of information.  

---

## Best Practices for the "Icon Cards" Component  

Follow these tips for the best results:  
- **Prioritize clarity:** Keep headings short and write simple text. This helps viewers quickly understand each card’s message.  
- **Use the right icons:** Choose icons that match the card's content to make the meaning clear at first glance.  
- **Don’t overload the design:** Aim for balance. If you have many cards, consider breaking them into smaller groups across multiple sections.  
- **Preview your section:** Make sure the layout looks good and the cards are evenly distributed.  

---

With these steps and tips, you’re ready to create eye-catching, user-friendly "Icon Cards" for your website. Happy editing! 😊
