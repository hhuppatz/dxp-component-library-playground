# User Guide: Editing the Icon Card – Single Component in Squiz Matrix

The **Icon Card – Single** component is a flexible and visually appealing element that lets you display content alongside an icon, a heading, and optional clickable links. Use this guide to learn how to edit the Icon Card component, what each field means, and tips for creating engaging content.

---

## What is the Icon Card – Single Component?

The **Icon Card – Single** component looks like a rectangular card that spans 100% of its container's width. The card consists of:

- **An icon**: A small visual element adding context or flair.
- **A heading**: A bold, eye-catching title.
- **Text content**: A short description or details about the card's purpose.
- **An optional link**: Makes the entire card clickable, directing users to another page or section.

### When Should You Use This Component?

Use the Icon Card – Single component to highlight a specific feature, service, or important information on your website in an engaging, clear, and structured way. For example:
- To display concise descriptions of tools or services next to their icons.
- To provide clickable call-to-action cards that guide users to other pages.

---

## Step-by-Step Editing Guide

Follow these steps to edit the Icon Card – Single component. Fields marked **(required)** must be filled out.

### 1. Add or Edit the Icon
The **Icon** field determines the small image displayed on your card.

1. Use the dropdown menu in the **Icon** field to select one of the predefined icons:
   - `paintBucket`
   - `pen`
   - `printer`
   - `tools`
2. Choose an icon that matches or complements the card content.

> **Tip:** Ensure the icon visually aligns with the card's heading and text for clarity.

---

### 2. Add a Heading (required)
The **Heading** is the title of your card.

1. Click into the **Heading** text box.
2. Enter or edit the title of your card. Use clear and concise language.
3. Keep it short and descriptive to grab attention (e.g., "Quick Setup").

> **Best Practice:** Use sentence case (capitalize only the first word and proper nouns) for a professional and clean look.

---

### 3. Add the Main Content Text (required)
The **Content** field is where you can describe the card's purpose.

1. Click into the **Content** or text area field.
2. Write a short message or description that supports the heading.
3. Use tools in the text editor to format text (e.g., bold or italics, if available). Avoid overly long descriptions.

> **Tip:** Two or three concise sentences are usually enough. Focus on what’s most important or helpful for your audience.

---

### 4. Add a Link (optional)
The **Link** field adds a clickable URL to the entire card.

1. If you'd like the card to link to another page or resource:
   - Add a URL (e.g., `https://example.com`) or select from available internal links using the dropdown.
2. This step is optional. If left blank, the card will not link anywhere.
   
> **Best Practice:** Ensure your link directs users to relevant, helpful content. Use clear call-to-action phrases in your heading or content (e.g., "Learn More" or "Discover Our Tools") to encourage clicks.

---

## Required vs. Optional Fields
Here's a summary of which fields are **required** and which are **optional**:

- **Icon:** Required – You must select an icon.
- **Heading:** Required – Add a descriptive title for the card.
- **Text Content:** Required – Fill in the main card description.
- **Link:** Optional – Only use this if you want your card to link somewhere.

---

## Best Practices for Great Icon Cards

- **Keep it Simple:** Ensure the heading and content are easy to understand, even at a glance. Short, impactful phrases work best.
- **Visual Consistency:** Pick an icon that matches the tone and message of your text. Avoid using icons that might confuse the user.
- **Effective Links:** If you add a link, make sure it’s relevant and gives users clarity about where they’ll be taken when they click.
- **Avoid Excessive Text:** Cards work best when they provide concise, scannable information.

---

By following these instructions and tips, you'll create effective and engaging Icon Cards that enhance your website and improve user experience. Have fun customizing your content!
