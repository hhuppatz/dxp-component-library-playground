# User Guide: Block Quote Component

Welcome! This guide will help you understand how to use and edit the **Block Quote** component in Squiz Matrix. Follow the steps below to create a visually attractive quote section for your web page.

---

## What is the Block Quote Component?

The **Block Quote** component lets you display a quote on your webpage, along with the author’s name, and optionally a title above the quote to give context. It’s a great way to highlight inspirational quotes, customer testimonials, or any other noteworthy text.

This component looks like this on the page:

- **Title**: A headline displayed above the quote (optional).
- **Quote**: The main text of the quote (required).
- **Author**: The name of the person who wrote or said the quote (optional).

### When to Use It:
- To emphasize a key message or testimonial from someone.
- To display inspirational or meaningful quotes in a visually appealing way.
- To add personality and credibility with author attribution.

---

## How to Fill Out the Fields

Follow these steps to edit the Block Quote component and create your own quote section:

### 1. **Title (Optional)**
   - **What It Does**: This is the heading text displayed above your quote. Use it to provide context or introduce the quote.
   - **Default Value**: "Section title"
   - **Steps to Edit**:
     1. Click on the Title field.
     2. Replace the default text ("Section title") with your desired heading.
     3. Keep this short and clear, ideally no longer than one sentence.

   *Tip*: Use a meaningful title that helps readers understand the relevance of the quote.

---

### 2. **Quote (Required)**
   - **What It Does**: The main text of the quote. This is what your audience will focus on.
   - **Steps to Edit**:
     1. Click on the Quote field.
     2. Type or paste your quote into the field.
     3. You can format the quote using bold, italics, or list items if needed.

   *Important*: This field is required, so make sure it’s filled in before publishing your page.

   *Tip*: Keep your quote concise and impactful. Avoid overly long quotes that may lose the reader’s attention.

---

### 3. **Author (Optional)**
   - **What It Does**: The name of the person the quote is attributed to. Adding an author adds credibility to the quote.
   - **Default Value**: "Author"
   - **Steps to Edit**:
     1. Click on the Author field.
     2. Replace “Author” with the correct name.
     3. If the author’s identity isn’t important or relevant, you can leave this blank.

   *Tip*: Use the full name of the author for a professional look. If the quote is by an organization or group, you can add their name instead.

---

## Field Overview

Here’s a quick reference table for the fields:

| **Field Name** | **Required/Optional** | **Description**                                                                 |
|-----------------|-----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Title           | Optional              | A short heading displayed above the quote. Default: "Section title".           |
| Quote           | Required              | The main text of the quote. Must be filled in before publishing the component.  |
| Author          | Optional              | The name of the person (or group) credited for the quote. Default: "Author".    |

---

## Best Practices for Beautiful Quotes

1. **Choose Short, Impactful Quotes**: Short quotes are easier to read and make a stronger impression. Avoid overly long quotes that might overwhelm users.
2. **Use a Relevant Title**: If you’re adding a title, keep it simple but descriptive so readers immediately understand why the quote is important.
3. **Double-Check Your Content**: Ensure the quote text is accurate and the author’s name is spelled correctly.
4. **Maintain Visual Balance**: Avoid combining very long titles with long quotes to ensure the Block Quote component looks clean and professional.
5. **Stay Consistent**: If you’re using multiple Block Quotes on a page, keep the formatting similar for all of them.

---

## Conditional Fields

There are no conditional fields in this component. All fields are always visible, making it easy to edit.

---

## Summary

The Block Quote component is perfect for making your content shine with beautifully presented quotes. Only the **Quote** field is required, but adding a **Title** and **Author** helps give it depth and context.

Follow the steps outlined above and use the tips to create visually appealing and compelling quotes for your web page.

Happy editing! 🎉
