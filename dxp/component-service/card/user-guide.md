# User Guide: Editing the "Card" Component in Squiz Matrix

Welcome to the user guide for the "Card" component! This guide will help you understand what the Card looks like on your webpage, what it is used for, and how to edit it step by step. Let’s make your content look amazing!

---

## What Does the Card Look Like?

The Card component is a versatile block of content that typically includes:

- A **content type label** displayed at the top (e.g., "News," "Blog Post," or "Event").
- A bold **heading** that acts as the main title or attention-grabber for the card.
- Additional **supporting text** that provides a brief description or context.
- An optional **background image** that makes the card more visually appealing.
- An optional **link**, such as a button, allowing users to navigate to another page or resource.

Cards are useful for showcasing key information in an organized and visually engaging way. Typical uses include news highlights, featured articles, or promotional content.

---

## How to Edit the Card Component

Follow these steps to customize your Card component:

### 1. Edit the **Content Type** (Optional)

   - **What is it?**  
     The "Content Type" is a small label you can use to categorize your card, such as "News" or "Event."  
   - **How to update it:**  
     1. Locate the **Content Type** field. 
     2. Enter a short label (e.g., "Blog Post").  
     3. Keep it concise – one or two words are ideal!  

   **Tips:**  
   - This field is optional. If you don’t need it, leave it blank.  

---

### 2. Edit the **Heading** (Required)

   - **What is it?**  
     The "Heading" is the main title of the card. This is a required field and should grab the user’s attention.  
   - **How to update it:**  
     1. Locate the **Heading** field.  
     2. Add your desired title. Aim for something descriptive yet concise (around 5-7 words).  

   **Tips:**  
   - This is required, so make sure to fill it out before saving.  
   - Use clear and engaging language to encourage users to explore further.  

---

### 3. Add **Supporting Text** (Optional)

   - **What is it?**  
     The "Supporting Text" gives users more information about the content of the card. It's displayed below the heading.  
   - **How to update it:**  
     1. Locate the **Supporting Text** field.  
     2. Write a brief description or highlight key details, keeping it under 2-3 sentences.  

   **Tips:**  
   - Keep the text easy to read and to the point.  
   - Use this field to provide any context users might need to understand the headline.  

---

### 4. Add a **Link** (Optional)

   - **What is it?**  
     The "Link" allows you to add a clickable button or hyperlink to the card so users can navigate to another page or perform an action.  
   - **How to update it:**  
     1. Locate the **Link** field.  
     2. Specify the link destination (e.g., another page on your site, a document, or an external link).  
     3. Write a brief and clear link text (e.g., "Read More" or "Register Now").  

   **Tips:**  
   - Be sure the link takes users to a relevant page.  
   - Use action-oriented text for the link that matches what the user will find (e.g., "Learn More" or "Get Details").  

---

### 5. Add an **Image** (Optional)

   - **What is it?**  
     The "Image" is the visual element displayed in the background of your card. It makes your card more attractive and engaging.  
   - **How to update it:**  
     1. Locate the **Image** field.  
     2. Click to upload or select an image from the library.  
     3. Use high-quality images that are relevant to your content.  

   **Tips:**  
   - Stick to images with good contrast so your text is easy to read.  
   - Check the dimensions of your card to ensure the image fits well.  

---

## Required vs. Optional Fields at a Glance

| Field            | Required?      | Notes                                                                 |
|------------------|----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Content Type** | Optional       | A short label to categorize the card (e.g., "News" or "Event").       |
| **Heading**      | Required       | The main title of the card – make it concise and engaging.            |
| **Supporting Text** | Optional   | Provides additional context; keep it brief.                           |
| **Link**         | Optional       | Add a link or button if the card should lead somewhere.               |
| **Image**        | Optional       | Adds visual appeal – ensure it’s clear and relevant.                  |

---

## Best Practices for Using the Card Component

1. **Keep it Simple**: Cards should be easy to scan. Use concise and impactful text.  
2. **Engage with Images**: A high-quality, relevant image can make your card much more effective.  
3. **Be Consistent**: If using multiple cards, ensure their layout and style are consistent across your site.  
4. **Prioritize Accessibility**: Make sure your link text is descriptive and avoid using vague terms like "Click Here."
