# User Guide: Editing the Image Text Row Component

Welcome to the user guide for the **Image Text Row** component. This guide will walk you through what this component looks like, how to customize it, and tips to help you make the most of its features.

---

## What is the Image Text Row Component?

The **Image Text Row** component is a versatile layout tool used to display a section consisting of an **image and text** side by side. It provides a simple, visually engaging way to present content on a webpage.

### How It Looks:

- The section has an **image** on one side (left or right) and **text content** on the other.
- The text content can include:
  - A **content type label** (a small label above the heading).
  - A **title** describing the section.
  - A **heading** to grab attention.
  - A descriptive **text block**.
  - An optional **link** for users to learn more.

You can choose whether the **image appears on the left or right side** of the text block.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions for Editing

Follow these simple steps to customize the **Image Text Row** component:

### 1. Add or Edit Content
1. Locate the **"Content"** section.
2. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Section Title** (Optional): Add a title if you want a heading displayed above the section. Example: "About Our Services".
   - **Image** (Required): Upload or select the image you want to appear in this section. Ensure the image is relevant and high-quality.
   - **Content Type** (Optional): Add a short label to describe the type of content. For instance, "Case Study", "News", or "Feature".
   - **Heading** (Required): Enter the main headline for the section. Example: "Why Choose Us?".
   - **Text Content** (Required): Write a brief description. This is your opportunity to explain your message clearly and concisely.
   - **Link** (Optional): Add a link if you want users to be directed to another page or resource. Example: "Learn More".

### 2. Configure the Layout
1. Locate the **"Configuration"** section.
2. Select the **Variant** (Required):
   - **Text-Left**: The text will appear on the left side, and the image will appear on the right.
   - **Text-Right**: The text will appear on the right side, and the image will appear on the left.

---

## Required vs Optional Fields

### Required Fields:
- In the **Content** section:
  - **Heading**: You must provide a headline for the component.
  - **Text Content**: You must provide the main text for this section.
  - **Image**: An image is also mandatory to ensure the layout looks complete and visually appealing.
  
- In the **Configuration** section:
  - **Variant**: You must select whether the text appears on the left or right side of the image.

### Optional Fields:
These fields are not required, but adding content here can enhance the presentation:
- **Section Title**
- **Content Type**
- **Link**

---

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Keep It Short and Impactful**  
   - Use concise and easy-to-digest text for your **Heading** and **Text Content**.
   - Make sure your **Section Title** and **Content Type** provide clear context for readers.

2. **Use High-Quality Images**  
   - Choose an image that is clear and relevant to the message.
   - Ensure the image file size is optimized for the web to ensure fast loading times.

3. **Make the Most of Links**  
   - Add a **Link** only if it's helpful to your audience (e.g., more information, a product page, or a call-to-action). Keep the linked text actionable (e.g., "Learn more", "Contact us", or "Get started").

4. **Consistent Layouts**  
   - Alternate the **Variant** between image-on-left and image-on-right for consecutive **Image Text Rows** to create visual interest.
   - If placing multiple Image Text Rows on the same page, ensure a consistent design style (e.g., image sizes and heading fonts).

5. **Preview and Test Your Changes**  
   - Before publishing, preview your page to see how the **Image Text Row** looks. Make sure the text, image, and link are visually appealing and align well.

---

## Conditional Fields: What to Watch Out For

- The **Link** field is **optional** and will not appear on the page if left blank.
- The layout of the component (image on left or right) depends on the **Variant** you select. When adjusting this setting, ensure the image and text content complement each other visually.

---
