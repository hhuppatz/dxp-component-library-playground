# User Guide: Editing the "Content Summary" Component in Squiz Matrix

Welcome! This guide will walk you through editing the **Content Summary** component in Squiz Matrix. This component is designed to help you deliver concise, impactful information to your audience. Let’s get started!

---

## What is the Content Summary Component?

The **Content Summary** component is a simple but versatile content block. It looks like this on the page:

- **Heading**: The title or main message you want to convey.
- **Text Content**: A short paragraph providing more details or supporting information.
- **Link**: An optional clickable link that directs users to another page for more information.
- **Content Type (Label)** (Optional): A small label displayed above the heading to categorize the content (e.g., "News", "Update", "Event").

### Example:
```
[Content Type: News]
[Heading: "Exciting Website Update!"]
[Text Content: "We’ve introduced a new design to make browsing easier."]
[Link: "Read more"]
```

Use this component to summarize new updates, highlight key information, or guide your audience to explore further information via linked pages.

---

## How to Edit the Content Summary Component

Follow these steps to edit your Content Summary component. You don’t need any coding experience—just fill out the editable fields!

### Step 1: Open the Content Summary Editor
1. Navigate to the page where the Content Summary component is placed.
2. Select the component to access its editing options.

---

### Step 2: Fill in the Editable Fields

Below is a breakdown of each field, how to use it, and whether it’s **required** or **optional**.

#### 1. **Content Type (Optional)**
   - **What it does**: A label that appears above the heading to classify your content.
   - **Examples**: "Blog", "News", "Event", "Update", "Announcement".
   - **How to edit**:
     1. Find the “Content Type” field.
     2. Replace the placeholder text ("Content Type") with your desired label text.
     3. Leave this field blank if you don’t want a label (it’s optional!).

   **Tip**: Keep the Content Type brief—1-2 words maximum. This makes it easier for users to scan the page.

---

#### 2. **Heading (Required)**
   - **What it does**: The main title or summary of your content.
   - **Examples**: "Upcoming Summer Events", "New Product Launch", "5 Tips for Better Time Management".
   - **How to edit**:
     1. Find the “Heading” field.
     2. Replace the placeholder text ("Heading") with your desired title.

   **Tip**: Keep your heading concise (5-10 words) and make it attention-grabbing!

---

#### 3. **Text Content (Required)**
   - **What it does**: A paragraph that provides more detail or context about the heading.
   - **Examples**:
     - "Join us this summer for exciting events hosted in your city!"
     - "We’re thrilled to announce the launch of our latest product, designed to make your life easier."
   - **How to edit**:
     1. Locate the “Text Content” field.
     2. Type in your description or information.

   **Tip**: Keep your text content short and to the point, with 1-2 sentences for easy readability.

---

#### 4. **Link (Optional)**
   - **What it does**: A clickable link under the text content, leading users to more detailed information, a sign-up page, or related resources.
   - **How to edit**:
     1. Find the “Link” field.
     2. Enter the destination URL.
     3. Optionally, you can edit the display text for the link (e.g., "Learn more", "Get started today").
     4. If you don’t want a link, leave this field blank.

   **Tip**: Use clear and action-oriented phrases for your link text to encourage clicks. For example, “Discover More” works better than just “Link.”

---

## Required vs. Optional Fields

To summarize:
- **Required Fields**:
  - Heading
  - Text Content
- **Optional Fields**:
  - Content Type
  - Link

If you don’t fill in the required fields, the component will not work properly.

## Best Practices for Using the Content Summary Component

Here are some tips for creating effective and engaging content summaries:

1. **Keep it Simple**  
   - The Content Summary component works best for short, clear messages. Use simple language and avoid long paragraphs.

2. **Use Actionable Headings**  
   - Write headings that spark curiosity or inspire action. For example, "Register for Our Free Webinar Today!" is more engaging than "Webinar Registration".

3. **Prioritize Clarity**  
   - Make sure the information is clear and tailored to your audience. Ask yourself: “If I read this, would I know what I need to do next?”

4. **Test Your Links**  
   - Always check that the link works properly and goes to the right destination.

5. **Avoid Overloading Content**  
   - If you have too much to say, use additional Content Summary components rather than overloading one section.

6. **Preview Before Publishing**  
   - Use the preview options to double-check how the component looks in the context of your webpage before publishing changes.
